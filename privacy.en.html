CookTutor Privacy Policy

Version: 11 September 2026. Same text as the in-app Privacy Policy.

What we do not collect

CookTutor does not use ads, third-party analytics, or tracking. We do not upload your recipes, nickname, or cooking records to a developer server. We do not sell or share that data. The core cook path does not require an account or a login wall.

Purchases (StoreKit)

Lifetime Unlock is a one-time Non-Consumable in-app purchase. Apple StoreKit handles payment and receipts. CookTutor only reads Apple’s entitlement to know whether the kitchen is unlocked for life. We do not upload payment details to a developer server. This is not a subscription and does not auto-renew. Use Restore Purchases after you change phones. Apple’s own privacy policy covers how Apple handles transaction data.

How data is stored

Personal recipes, nickname, favourites, This Meal, and the grocery list stay on the device. During the trial and after you buy Lifetime Unlock, they can sync through your Apple ID into your private iCloud store. The developer cannot browse that private store. If several devices write the same record, the last successful write wins. If you are not signed in to iCloud, changes stay on this device only. When the trial ends and you have not purchased, the kitchen locks; data already on this device remains, and you can keep using it after restore or purchase. In App Store privacy labels, “Name” is the nickname you type and “Other User Content” is the recipes and cooking records you write. Both are used for app functionality, not tracking.

Approximate location

When you tap Plan a Meal for Me, Choose Another Meal, or Swap This Dish, CookTutor may read approximate location at most once every 30 days to judge the season. Opening This Meal does not request location. Precise coordinates are not saved and are not uploaded to developer servers. A coarse region is saved on this device in your profile. During the trial and after Lifetime Unlock, it can sync with your profile into your private iCloud store. The developer cannot browse that store. If you decline, the phone region is used instead. This matches the App Store privacy label for Coarse Location: app functionality only, not tracking.

Web import

The app fetches a recipe page only when you paste an HTTPS URL, and it parses that page on the device. The destination site may log the visit under its own policy.

Cover photos

Downloaded official plate photos sit in the system cache, are not backed up to iCloud, and may be purged when storage is low.

Profile photo

The profile photo you choose stays on this device. Location data is stripped before saving. CookTutor does not upload profile photos to the developer’s servers.

Notifications and deletion

Timers send alerts only after you grant notification permission. You can turn notifications off in Settings. Personal recipes can be deleted in the app; iCloud copies can be removed through your Apple ID’s iCloud tools.

Children

CookTutor does not knowingly collect personal data from children and does not offer public chat, stranger interaction, or targeted ads.

Contact

For privacy help, use App Support on the App Store product page. See Support.
