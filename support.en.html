CookTutor · home cooking you can follow even if you are new to the kitchen

How to reach us

Use App Support on the App Store product page. Recipe requests, feature ideas, and bugs can also go in an App Store review — the same note lives in the app under Me → A Note from Us. Until a public email is published, do not invent a contact address in the repo or review notes.

What the app does

Official recipes, Cook along, This Meal, and the grocery list are free and need no account. My Kitchen is a one-time purchase for unlimited private recipes and iCloud. The Mac editorial tools are not in the iPhone app.

Purchases

My Kitchen is a one-time buyout, not a subscription. Terms are Apple’s standard EULA. After switching devices, use Restore Purchases in the app.
