CookTutor App Support

饭有方 · Home cooking you can follow

How to use

CookTutor is a follow-along kitchen: Official Recipes, Start Cooking, This Meal, the grocery list, and My Recipes. The core cook path does not require an account or a login wall. Mac editorial tools are not in the iPhone app.

Language

Switch language in Me → Settings → 语言 / Language. The Simplified Chinese name is 饭有方, Traditional Chinese is 飯有方, and English is CookTutor. The product languages are Simplified Chinese, Hong Kong Traditional, Taiwan Traditional, and British English.

Trial, purchase, and restore

From first launch you get a 7-day trial of the full kitchen. When the trial ends the kitchen locks until you buy Lifetime Unlock once (Non-Consumable in-app purchase com.cooktutor.kitchen.lifetime). One purchase unlocks the kitchen for life, including after you change phones. This is not a subscription and does not auto-renew. The price is the one shown in the app and on the App Store (US storefront reference: US $14.99). After a new phone or reinstall, use Me → Lifetime Unlock → Restore Purchases.

How to contact us

Use App Support on the App Store product page. Recipe requests, feature ideas, and bugs can also go in an App Store review — the same note lives in the app under Me → A Note from Us. There is no public community and no developer-hosted account. Do not invent an email address in the repository or review notes until one is published.
